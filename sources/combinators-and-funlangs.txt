PREFACE

This volume presents the Proceedings of the Thirteenth Spring School of the LITP (Laboratoire
Informatique Throrique et Programmation, Universitrs Paris VI-VII, CNRS), held May 6-10,
1985 in Val d'Ajol (a village in the Vosges, France). The subject was "Combinators and Functional
Programming Languages". Our aim in organizing this school was to cover a quite wide spectrum
from foundations to implementation techniques. The collected papers, mostly tutorial, cover
an introduction to the different available formalisms of functions: lambda-calculus and
Curry's combinators, Cartesian closed categories; the papers emphasize the connection between
categories and proof theory, between proof theory and type systems for programming languages
(the Curry-Howard isomorphism);
-

- a description of some new, efficient implementation techniques: the Amber machine and
the Categorical abstract machine (CAM) are derivatives of Landin's SECD-machine and differ in
the way of representing environments (arrays against linked environments); another technique is
graph reduction of (super)-combinators;
two illustrations of the elegance and expressive power of functional programming
-

languages: Amber (which incorporates inheritance and parallelism) and Graal (a language without
variables, ~ la Backus).
However the volume does not cover two conferences of the Spring School: one by C.
Boehm on the Applications of the second order lambda-calculus to program synthesis, and one by
T. Coquand and G. Huet on the Calculus of constructions, a powerful typed tambda-calculus,
well-suited for the mechanization of mathematical reasoning.
For further reading we recommend the following monographs:
