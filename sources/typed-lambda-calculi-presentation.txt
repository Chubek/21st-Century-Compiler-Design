WEAK NORMALIZABILITY

Weak normalizability

In the untyped λ-calculus it is undecidable whether a term is weakly normalizing, i.e. whether it reduces to a normal term. This changes dramatically
with types: all terms have a normal form.
Some useful definitions.
• The degree of a type is defined by
d(o) = 0
d(A → B) = 1 + max(d(A), d(B))
• The degree of a redex (λxA .M )N is the degree of the type of λxA .M .
• The degree of a term is equal to the highest degree of a redex occurring
in it.
