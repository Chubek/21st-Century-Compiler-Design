Abstract
In this paper we present a non-deterministic
call-by-need
(untyped)
lambda calculus X,d with a constant choice and a
let-syntax
that models sharing. Our main result is that Xnd
has the nice operational

confluence
on sets of expressions,
and normal order reduction
is sufficient

Using a strong contextual

In particular
of lambdalifting using deterministic
maximal
free expressions.
These

of non-determinism

which has a lot of

An intended application

The intention

On the one hand a
